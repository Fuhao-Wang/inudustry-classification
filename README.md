Dean, Judith M., and Mary E. Lovely. 2010. “Trade Growth, Production Fragmentation, and China’s Environment.” In *China’s Growing Role in World Trade*, edited by Robert C. Feenstra and Shang-Jin Wei. Chicago: University of Chicago Press for the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER).
